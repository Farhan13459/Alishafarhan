Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shipping, payments, returns, and more

Shipping & Delivery

What are your delivery times?

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days. Express delivery is available for 1-2 business days at an additional cost. International shipping may take 7-14 business days depending on the destination.

How much does shipping cost?

Free standard shipping on all orders over $50. For orders below $50, standard shipping is $4.99. Express shipping is $9.99 for all orders.

Do you offer international shipping?

Yes, we ship to over 50 countries worldwide. International shipping costs vary by destination and are calculated at checkout. Delivery times typically range from 7-14 business days.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can track your package using our order tracking page or directly with the carrier.

What if I'm not home during delivery?

For standard delivery, packages will be left in a safe place. For high-value items requiring signature, the carrier will attempt delivery 3 times before returning to sender. You can also redirect to a pickup location.

Payments & Security

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and bank transfers. All payments are processed securely through encrypted channels.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes, we use industry-standard SSL encryption to protect your payment information. We never store your complete payment details on our servers. All transactions are processed through PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways.

Do you offer installment plans?

Yes, we offer installment plans through PayPal Credit and Affirm for orders over $100. You can choose installment options at checkout with 0% interest available on select items.

Why was my payment declined?

Payment declines can occur due to insufficient funds, incorrect card information, security checks by your bank, or international transaction restrictions. Please contact your bank or try a different payment method.

When will my card be charged?

Your card is authorized when you place the order, but the actual charge occurs when your order ships. For pre-orders, you'll be charged 1-2 days before the release date.

Returns & Refunds

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day return policy for most items. Products must be unused, in original packaging with all tags attached. Some items like electronics have specific return conditions.

How do I return an item?

Start a return through your account page, print the return label, and ship the item back to us. Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll process your refund within 3-5 business days.

How long do refunds take?

Refunds are processed within 3-5 business days after we receive your return. It may take 5-10 additional business days for the refund to appear in your account, depending on your bank.

Do you offer exchanges?

Yes, we offer size and color exchanges for most items. If the exchange item is available, we'll ship it immediately. Otherwise, we'll process a refund and you can place a new order.

Account & Support

How do I create an account?

Click the "Sign Up" button in the top navigation, enter your email address, create a password, and verify your email. You can also create an account during checkout.

I forgot my password. How can I reset it?

Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email address, and we'll send you a password reset link. The link expires in 2 hours for security.

How do I contact customer support?

You can reach our support team via email at support@smartshop.com, through our live chat during business hours (9 AM - 6 PM EST), or by calling 1-800-SMART-SHOP.
